        About The Campus Chronicle

        Your Campus. Your Stories. Your Voice.

        📰

        Our Mission

        Founded in 1978, The Campus Chronicle is Crestwood University's award-winning student-run digital newspaper. We provide accurate, timely, and engaging journalism that reflects the diverse voices of our campus community.

        🎓

        Student-Led

        Our 40-member editorial team comprises undergraduate and postgraduate students from all faculties. We believe in training the next generation of journalists through hands-on, real-world reporting experience.

        🏆

        Our Achievements

        Winner of the National College Press Association "Best Digital Newspaper" award three years running. Recognised by the Press Freedom Foundation for our commitment to independent, fearless journalism.

        🤝

        Join Our Team

        We are always looking for passionate writers, photographers, designers, and videographers. Reach out to us at join@campuschronicle.edu to apply for the upcoming semester intake.
